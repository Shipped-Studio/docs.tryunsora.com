---
title: "Upscale video"
description: "Upscale a video up to 25 seconds long with the standard, Ultra 1080p or Ultra 4K model."
openapi: "POST /video-upscaler/create"
---

Upscales a video up to 25 seconds long. The price comes from the video's measured length:

| `model` | Up to 5 s | Up to 10 s | Longer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `standard` (default) | 10 | 10 | 10 |
| `ultra-1080p` | 6 | 11 | 16 |
| `ultra-4k` | 22 | 43 | 64 |

Poll [Poll video status](/api-reference/video/poll-status) with `job.id`.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://mvp.tryunsora.com/api/v1/video-upscaler/create" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer uns_YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{ "videoUrl": "https://cdn.example.com/clip.mp4", "model": "ultra-1080p" }'
```
