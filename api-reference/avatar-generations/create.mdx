---
title: "Talking avatar"
description: "Turn a portrait into a talking video that speaks your script or an audio file, up to 20 seconds. Costs 10 credits."
openapi: "POST /avatar-generations/create"
---

Animates a portrait so it speaks. Send either a `transcript` (read in `voice_id`) or an `audio_url`; either way the speech can be up to 20 seconds. Costs 10 credits.

`voice_id` takes a MiniMax preset, an Eleven v3 voice or one of your cloned voices; see [List voices you can use](/api-reference/voice-clones/list). Poll [Poll video status](/api-reference/video/poll-status) with `generation.id`.

```bash
curl -X POST "https://mvp.tryunsora.com/api/v1/avatar-generations/create" \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer uns_YOUR_API_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{
    "image_url": "https://cdn.example.com/portrait.jpg",
    "transcript": "Hi, I'm Maya. Here are three tips for better morning routines.",
    "voice_id": "Calm_Woman",
    "emotion": "happy",
    "resolution": "720p"
  }'
```
