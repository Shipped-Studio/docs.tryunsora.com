---
title: "Create Post"
description: "Schedule or draft posts to YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Threads, Pinterest and Google Business Profile."
openapi: "POST /posts"
---

Schedule a post for later or save it as a **draft**. A scheduled post is published at its time by the Unsora scheduler; a draft is published with [Publish post](/api-reference/posts/publish).

Posts can target any connected account on **YouTube, TikTok, Instagram,
Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Threads, Pinterest and Google Business
Profile**: up to 10 accounts per post, mixed platforms allowed, each account
once.

## Supported content

| `media` | Post type | Use for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `{ "type": "video", … }` | `VIDEO` | Reels, Shorts, TikToks, feed videos |
| `{ "type": "slideshow", "urls": […] }` | `CAROUSEL` | Instagram carousels, TikTok photo posts, multi-image posts |
| An array of media items | Inferred: `VIDEO` if any item is a video, otherwise `CAROUSEL` for 2+ images or `IMAGE` for one | Mixed cases, single images, videos with a cover |
| Omitted or `null` | `TEXT` | Text-only posts |

Which post types each platform takes:

| Platform | Video | Image / carousel | Text only |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| YouTube | ✅ | — | — |
| TikTok, Instagram, Pinterest | ✅ | ✅ | — |
| Facebook, LinkedIn, X, Bluesky, Threads | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Google Business Profile | — | ✅ (one image) | ✅ |

Connect accounts in the Unsora app, then list them with
[Get accounts](/api-reference/accounts/get_accounts).

## Media requirements

A scheduled post is checked when you create it. If the media doesn't fit a
platform, the request fails with `400` and code `PLATFORM_RULES`, with one
entry per problem in `issues`. Drafts are checked when you publish them.

| Platform | Caption limit | Images per post | Video length | Video size |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Instagram | 2,200 | 10 | 3 s – 15 min | 300 MB |
| TikTok | 2,200 | 35 | 3 s – 10 min | — |
| YouTube | 5,000 (description) | — | — | — |
| Facebook | 63,206 | 10 | — | — |
| LinkedIn | 3,000 | 20 | 3 s – 30 min | 500 MB |
| X | 280 | 4 | up to 140 s | 512 MB |
| Threads | 500 | 20 | up to 5 min | 1 GB |
| Bluesky | 300 characters | 4 | up to 3 min | 100 MB |
| Pinterest | 800 | 5 | 4 s – 15 min | 2 GB |
| Google Business Profile | 1,500 | 1 | — | — |

Image format, size and aspect ratio don't block a post. Unsora fixes them for
each platform at publish time:

- **Format:** images a platform doesn't take are converted to JPEG (Instagram takes JPEG only).
- **Aspect ratio:** images outside the allowed range (Instagram: 4:5 to 1.91:1) are padded over a blurred copy of themselves, nothing is cropped.
- **Carousels:** Instagram carousel images are padded to the first image's ratio, and Pinterest images are made the same pixel size.
- **Size:** oversized images are scaled and re-compressed under the platform's cap.

Videos should be MP4 or MOV (H.264 + AAC) at a publicly reachable URL.

## Before you post

1. Active **paid plan** + API key.
2. [Get accounts](/api-reference/accounts/get_accounts) → copy each account `id`.
3. Pick your media. For Pinterest, get a board id with [Pinterest boards](/api-reference/accounts/pinterest-boards); for TikTok, check the allowed privacy options with [TikTok creator info](/api-reference/accounts/tiktok-creator-info).

## Request body

<ParamField body="caption" type="string" required>
  Post caption. Legacy alias: `mainCaption`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="accounts" type="array" required>
  `[{ "id": "<account-id>" }]`: 1 to 10 accounts, each once (alias `accountId`). Each account object also accepts:

  - `customCaption`: replaces `caption` for that account only.
  - `title`: the **YouTube** video title. Defaults to the first 100 characters of the caption. Pinterest ignores it; use `settings.pinterest.title`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="media" type="object | array | null">
  <Expandable title="Video">
    <ParamField body="type" type="string" required>`video`</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="url" type="string" required>MP4/MOV URL reachable by Unsora.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="duration_sec" type="number">Video length in seconds.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="width" type="integer" />
    <ParamField body="height" type="integer" />
    <ParamField body="bytes" type="integer">File size in bytes.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="mime_type" type="string">e.g. `video/mp4`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="cover_url" type="string">
      Optional **Instagram Reel** cover image URL. Ignored by TikTok.
    </ParamField>
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Slideshow">
    <ParamField body="type" type="string" required>`slideshow`</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="urls" type="string[]" required>1–35 image URLs in order.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Array of media items">
    Each item: `type` (`VIDEO`, `IMAGE` or `THUMBNAIL`, where `THUMBNAIL` is a video cover), `url`, and optional `order`, `width`, `height`, `duration`, `fileSize`, `mimeType`.
  </Expandable>

  Omit, or send `null`, for a text post. Media URLs are stored as uploads and appear in [List uploads](/api-reference/uploads/list).
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="type" type="string">
  Overrides the inferred post type: `VIDEO`, `IMAGE`, `CAROUSEL` or `TEXT`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="settings" type="object">
  Optional per-platform options. You can also send `instagram`, `tiktok`, `youtube`, `pinterest` or `google_business` at the **top level** of the body (same shape). Each account gets only its own platform's options. Other keys under `settings` return `400`.

  <Expandable title="Instagram: settings.instagram">
    <ParamField body="cover_url" type="string">
      Custom **Reel** cover image URL. Same as `media.cover_url`.
    </ParamField>
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="TikTok: settings.tiktok">
    <ParamField body="privacy_level" type="string">
      `PUBLIC_TO_EVERYONE` (default), `MUTUAL_FOLLOW_FRIENDS`, `FOLLOWER_OF_CREATOR` or `SELF_ONLY`. Must be an option TikTok allows for the account; see [TikTok creator info](/api-reference/accounts/tiktok-creator-info).
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="disable_comment" type="boolean" />
    <ParamField body="disable_duet" type="boolean">Video posts only.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="disable_stitch" type="boolean">Video posts only.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="brand_content_toggle" type="boolean">Paid partnership (third-party brand).</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="brand_organic_toggle" type="boolean">Promoting the creator's own business.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="is_aigc" type="boolean">Label the post as AI-generated.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="post_mode" type="string">
      Photo posts only. `DIRECT_POST` (default) publishes; `MEDIA_UPLOAD` sends it to the creator's TikTok inbox to finish in the app.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="auto_add_music" type="boolean">Photo posts only. TikTok adds background music.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="photo_cover_index" type="integer">Photo posts only. Cover image index (0-based).</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="video_cover_timestamp_ms" type="integer">Video posts only. Cover frame time in ms. Default: `1000`.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="music_usage_confirmation" type="boolean">Stored with the post; not sent to TikTok.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="YouTube: settings.youtube">
    <ParamField body="privacy_status" type="string">
      `public` (default), `private`, or `unlisted`.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="tags" type="string[]">Video tags.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="category_id" type="string">YouTube category id. Default: `22` (People & Blogs).</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="made_for_kids" type="boolean">COPPA made-for-kids flag. Default: `false`.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Pinterest: settings.pinterest">
    <ParamField body="board_id" type="string">
      Board to pin to. Defaults to the account's first board.
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="title" type="string">Pin title, up to 100 characters. Defaults to the start of the caption.</ParamField>
    <ParamField body="link" type="string">Destination URL opened when the pin is clicked.</ParamField>
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Google Business Profile: settings.google_business">
    <ParamField body="cta_type" type="string">
      Button on the post: `LEARN_MORE`, `BOOK`, `ORDER`, `SHOP`, `SIGN_UP` or `CALL` (dials the location's phone number).
    </ParamField>
    <ParamField body="cta_url" type="string">
      Link the button opens. Required for every `cta_type` except `CALL`; leave it out for `CALL`.
    </ParamField>
  </Expandable>
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="scheduled_at" type="string">
  ISO 8601 with a time zone offset (or Unix time in milliseconds), **at least 2 minutes** ahead. Omit for a **draft**. Alias: `scheduledFor`.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="timezone" type="string">
  IANA time zone, e.g. `Europe/Berlin`. Stored with the schedule for display; it doesn't change how `scheduled_at` is read.
</ParamField>

<ParamField body="external_id" type="string">
  Your own id for the request. Without an `Idempotency-Key` header, its first 128 characters are used as the idempotency key.
</ParamField>

## Instagram reel cover

Only **`cover_url`**, a public image URL:

```json
"media": {
  "type": "video",
  "url": "https://cdn.example.com/reel.mp4",
  "cover_url": "https://cdn.example.com/cover.jpg"
}
```

TikTok reel cover frame: optional `settings.tiktok.video_cover_timestamp_ms`.

## Platform settings (optional)

Nothing in `settings` is required; leave it out if you don't need it. The YouTube video title goes on the **account object** (`accounts[].title`); the Pinterest pin title goes in `settings.pinterest.title`.

<Tip>
  Top-level `tiktok` / `instagram` / `youtube` / `pinterest` / `google_business` keys work the same as their `settings.*` equivalents.
</Tip>

```json
"settings": {
  "instagram": { "cover_url": "https://cdn.example.com/cover.jpg" },
  "tiktok": {
    "privacy_level": "SELF_ONLY",
    "is_aigc": true
  },
  "youtube": {
    "privacy_status": "unlisted",
    "tags": ["ai", "shorts"]
  },
  "pinterest": {
    "board_id": "1234567890",
    "title": "Weekend outfit ideas",
    "link": "https://example.com/lookbook"
  },
  "google_business": {
    "cta_type": "BOOK",
    "cta_url": "https://example.com/book"
  }
}
```

## Post lifecycle

`DRAFT` → `SCHEDULED` → `PUBLISHING` → one of:

| Status | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PUBLISHED` | Every account published |
| `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` | Some accounts published, some failed |
| `FAILED` | No account published |

For `FAILED` or `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED` posts, call
[Retry post](/api-reference/posts/retry): it re-attempts only the failed
accounts and never double-posts. Per-account results (including
`publishedUrl` and `error`) are on each post's `postAccounts`.

## Errors

| HTTP | When |
| --- | --- |
| `400` | Invalid body (missing caption, more than 10 accounts, a repeated account, `scheduled_at` less than 2 minutes ahead, an unknown settings key), or a scheduled post that fails a check: `MEDIA_REQUIRED`, `MEDIA_NOT_ALLOWED`, `MIXED_MEDIA`, `CAPTION_REQUIRED`, `UNSUPPORTED_FORMAT`, `PLATFORM_RULES` (see `issues`) or `PLATFORM_UNAVAILABLE` |
| `401` | Invalid auth |
| `403` | `PLAN_REQUIRED` (no paid plan) or `UNKNOWN_ACCOUNTS` (an account isn't yours) |
| `404` | User not found |
| `409` | `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT` |
| `500` | Server error |
