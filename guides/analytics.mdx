description: "How Unsora tracks views, likes, comments, shares and saves on your published posts, how often it refreshes them, and how to read them over the API."
---

Unsora reads engagement on every post it published for you and shows it under **Analytics** in the dashboard. Your agent can read the same numbers over MCP (`get_post_analytics`) or the API.

## What's tracked

Each published post gets **views**, **likes**, **comments**, **shares** and **saves**, where the platform provides them:

| Platform | What Unsora reads |
| --- | --- |
| TikTok | Views, likes, comments, shares |
| Instagram | Likes and comments; views, shares and saves through Instagram insights |
| YouTube | Views, likes, comments |
| Facebook | Reactions (counted as likes), comments, shares, impressions and video views |
| Threads | Views, likes, replies, reposts and quotes |
| Bluesky | Likes, replies, reposts and quotes (Bluesky has no view counts) |
| Pinterest | Impressions, saves, clicks |
| X | Impressions, likes, replies, reposts and quotes, bookmarks |
| LinkedIn | Reactions (counted as likes); impressions aren't available |
| Google Business Profile | Not tracked — Google no longer reports per-post stats |

## How often numbers refresh

Platforms don't push engagement to Unsora, so Unsora re-reads it on a schedule that slows down as a post ages:

| Post age | Re-read about every |
| --- | --- |
| First 48 hours | 6 hours |
| Up to 7 days | 24 hours |
| Up to 30 days | 3 days |
| Up to 90 days | Weekly |

After 90 days the numbers stop updating.

**X** is read less often: every 12 hours for the first 48 hours, every 2 days up to 7 days, then weekly up to 30 days, after which it stops.

Clicking **Update stats** in the dashboard, or calling the refresh endpoint, re-reads posts straight away, except ones read in the last 15 minutes (3 hours for X).

## Over the API

- `GET /posts/analytics/summary?days=30` — totals and per-post numbers for the last 7–90 days (default 30)
- `POST /posts/analytics/refresh` — re-read your posts now
- [Get post](/api-reference/posts/get_post) — one post's per-account results
