title: "Connect accounts"
description: "Connect TikTok, Instagram, YouTube and other social accounts to Unsora, share a connect link with a client, and reconnect accounts that expire."
---

Unsora posts through social accounts you connect once in the dashboard. After that, your agent, the API and the integrations can all post to them.

## Connect an account

1. Open **Accounts** in the dashboard sidebar.
2. Click the platform you want.
3. Click **Connect**, or **Copy link** to send it to whoever owns the account (see below).
4. Approve access on the platform, then come back to Unsora.

The account appears under **Connected accounts** with its profile picture and when it last posted.

## Platform notes

- **Instagram** needs a business or creator account. Connect it directly, or choose **Instagram via Facebook** if it's linked to a Facebook Page you manage.
- **Facebook** connects Pages you manage, not personal profiles.
- **LinkedIn** connects your personal profile. Company pages aren't supported yet.
- **Google Business Profile** connects every location you manage.
- **Bluesky** asks for your handle first; custom domains like `yourname.com` work.
- Platforms that aren't live yet show as **Coming soon**.

## Connect someone else's account

Managing a client's or teammate's account? In the connect dialog, click **Copy link** and send it to them. They approve access on their own device, and the account is added to your Unsora. The link works for **1 hour**. Bluesky can't be shared this way, because its sign-in starts from the account's handle.

## How many accounts

You can connect more than one account per platform. How many you can connect in total depends on your plan:

| Plan | Social accounts |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 5 |
| Growth | 15 |
| Scale | 50 |

Without a plan you can still connect up to the Starter limit, so your accounts are ready when you start a trial. Posting needs a plan; see [Plans and credits](/guides/plans-and-credits).

## Reconnect

Platforms expire access from time to time. An account that needs attention shows **Expires soon** or **Reconnect needed**; click it and approve access again. Posts to an account that needs reconnecting fail until you do, and you can retry them afterwards.

## Use connected accounts

- In the dashboard, pick accounts in the post composer ([Scheduler](/guides/scheduler)).
- Over the API, list them with [List accounts](/api-reference/accounts/get_accounts) and pass their ids to [Create post](/api-reference/posts/create_post).
- From an agent, ask it to list your accounts; it calls `get_accounts`.
