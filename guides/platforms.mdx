Image size limits: Instagram 8 MB, Facebook 10 MB, Pinterest 20 MB, X 5 MB, Threads 8 MB, Bluesky about 1 MB, Google Business Profile 5 MB.

A dash means Unsora doesn't add its own check; the platform's own rules still apply.

## What Unsora fixes for you

Before publishing, Unsora adjusts images that don't fit a platform, so the post goes through instead of failing:

- **Formats** — images in a format the platform doesn't take are converted to JPEG (Instagram only accepts JPEG). TikTok photos are always converted.
- **Aspect ratio** — images outside the allowed ratio are padded over a blurred copy of themselves; nothing is cropped. Instagram allows 4:5 to 1.91:1, Threads 1:10 to 10:1.
- **Carousels** — on Instagram every image is padded to the first image's ratio; on Pinterest every image is resized to the same size.
- **Size** — oversized images are scaled down and re-compressed under the platform's limit.

Images that already fit are left alone. If a fix fails, the original image is sent and the platform decides.

## Account requirements

- **Instagram** needs a business or creator account. You can connect it directly, or through Facebook if it's linked to a Facebook Page you manage.
- **Facebook** posts to Pages you manage, not personal profiles.
- **LinkedIn** posts to your personal profile. Company pages aren't supported yet.
- **Google Business Profile** connects every location you manage. Posts appear on your Google Search and Maps listing.
- **Bluesky** signs in with your handle; custom domains work too.

## Platform settings

Each platform has its own optional settings on a post:

- **YouTube** — title (required, up to 100 characters), privacy (public, unlisted or private), tags, category, made for kids
- **TikTok** — privacy, comments, duet and stitch, branded content labels, AI-generated label, auto-add music, cover frame
- **Instagram** — cover image for Reels
- **Pinterest** — board (the account's first board if you don't pick one), pin title (up to 100 characters), link
- **Google Business Profile** — call-to-action button (Learn more, Book, Order, Shop, Sign up, Call) and its link

See [Create post](/api-reference/posts/create_post) for the exact field names.
