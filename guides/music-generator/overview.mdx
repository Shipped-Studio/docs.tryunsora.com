The **Music generator** turns lyrics and a style into full songs powered by [Mureka AI](https://wavespeed.ai/models/mureka-ai). Open it from the dashboard sidebar at [`/music-generator`](https://app.tryunsora.com/music-generator).

## Layout

The page has two panels:

1. **Song settings** (left on desktop) — model, lyrics, style, and the generate button
2. **Results** (right) — compact cards for in-progress and completed tracks

On mobile, settings stack above results.

## Choose a model first

The **Model** selector is at the top of the song settings panel. Pick the engine before you write lyrics.

| Model | Best for | Credits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Mureka V9 (auto)** | Default full song with vocals | 3 |
| **Mureka V9**, **Mureka V8** | Full songs with lyrics | 3 |
| **Mureka V7.6** | Full songs with lyrics | 4 |
| **Mureka O2** | Full songs with lyrics | 6 |
| **Mureka V7.5 (background music)** | Instrumental background music | 4 |

<Note>
  Song models need **Lyrics**. **Mureka V7.5 (background music)** needs a **Style** or lyrics, and lyrics are optional.
</Note>

## Write lyrics and style

**Lyrics** — paste or write verse/chorus text. Section labels like `[Verse]` and `[Chorus]` are supported.

**Style** (optional for songs, needed for background music without lyrics) — plain language for genre, tempo, mood, and vocal style, for example: `r&b, slow, passionate, male vocal`.

Click **Generate song** (or **Generate music** for V7.5). Credits are deducted when the job is queued.

## Use your cloned voice

Turn on **Use my cloned voice** and pick one of your cloned voices. Unsora makes an instrumental track, then your cloned voice reads the lyrics over it. It speaks the lyrics; it doesn't sing them. The button changes to **Generate with my voice**. Lyrics are required.

Create cloned voices with the voice clone tools first.

## Results cards

Each result is a compact dashboard row:

- **Play / pause** on the artwork button
- **Title** from your style (or “Untitled song”)
- **One-line lyric preview** when idle
- **Rolling synced lyrics** while that track is the active player (see [Playback & player](/guides/music-generator/playback))
- **Download** and **delete** on hover (always visible on touch devices)

In-progress jobs show a spinner and status (`Queued`, `Generating…`). Failed jobs show the error message and can be dismissed.

## Tips

- Keep lyrics under 3,000 characters and the style under 1,024 characters.
- Add a style even when it's optional — it steers genre and vocal delivery.
- You can start playback and switch to other tools; audio keeps playing in the global player.
