**ClipCast** turns long videos into short clips from a Claude conversation.
Give Claude a podcast, stream, webinar, or interview, and ClipCast finds the
best moments and cuts them into Shorts, Reels, and TikToks, with captions if
you want them. Ask for specific moments in plain language, such as "every time
we talk about pricing".

When your clips are ready, Claude can post them for you: publish now, schedule
for later, or send one clip to several platforms at once. It can also show how
your posted clips are performing.

ClipCast works in Claude on the web, Claude Desktop, Claude mobile, and Claude
Code.

## What you need

- An Unsora account. Sign up at [tryunsora.com](https://tryunsora.com).
- Unsora credits for clipping.
- To post clips: a paid Unsora plan and at least one social account connected
  in the [Unsora web app](https://app.tryunsora.com). Supported platforms are
  YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky,
  X, and Google Business Profile.

## Connect ClipCast to Claude

1. In Claude, open **Settings** > **Connectors** and select **Browse connectors**.
2. Search for **ClipCast** and select **Connect**.
3. Sign in to Unsora and approve access.

Claude now lists the Unsora tools in any conversation. Turn the connector on or
off per conversation from the **+** menu > **Connectors**.

<Note>
  On Team and Enterprise plans, an organization Owner may need to enable the
  connector before members can use it.
</Note>

### Add it by URL

If you don't see the connector in the directory, add it as a custom connector:

1. Open **Settings** > **Connectors** and select **Add custom connector**.
2. Enter `https://claude-mcp.tryunsora.com/mcp` as the URL.
3. Select **Add**, then **Connect**, and sign in to Unsora.

For Claude Code, run:

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http clipcast https://claude-mcp.tryunsora.com/mcp
```

Then run `/mcp` in Claude Code and choose **Authenticate**.

## Example prompts

- "Turn this podcast into five 9:16 clips with captions: https://example.com/episode-42.mp4"
- "Find every moment where we talk about pricing in this webinar and clip them: https://example.com/webinar.mp4"
- "Cut this stream into 30-second highlights for YouTube Shorts: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID"
- "Post the second clip to TikTok and Instagram tomorrow at 9am with the caption 'New episode out now'."
- "How did my clips perform over the last 30 days?"

In Claude on the web and on desktop, your clips play in an interactive preview
right in the conversation. Posts, the post composer, and analytics appear the
same way.

## What Claude can do

| Tool | What it does | Changes data |
| ---- | ------------ | ------------ |
| `create_clipping` | Clips a long video into short clips | Yes, spends credits |
| `wait_for_clipping` | Checks a clipping job until it finishes | No |
| `get_accounts` | Lists your connected social accounts | No |
| `get_credits` / `get_subscription` | Shows your plan and credit balance | No |
| `compose_post` | Opens a post composer you finish yourself | Yes, when you submit it |
| `create_post` | Saves a draft, schedules, or publishes a post | Yes |
| `publish_post` | Publishes a draft or scheduled post now | Yes |
| `update_post` | Edits or reschedules a draft or scheduled post | Yes |
| `retry_post` | Retries the accounts that failed on a post | Yes |
| `delete_post` | Deletes a post from Unsora | Yes |
| `get_post` / `list_posts` | Reads your posts and their status | No |
| `get_post_analytics` | Shows views, likes, comments, and shares | No |
| `upload_file` | Imports media from a public URL into your library | Yes |
| `list_uploads` / `list_generations` | Browses your uploads and past results | No |
| `delete_generation` | Permanently deletes an item from your library | Yes |

Claude asks for your approval before running tools that change data, unless
you've allowed them to run without asking. Deleting a post in Unsora doesn't
remove content that's already published on a platform.

## Troubleshooting

**Claude says the connector isn't authorized.** Open **Settings** >
**Connectors**, select **ClipCast**, and choose **Disconnect**. Then
connect again and sign in.

**A post fails with "requires an active paid plan".** Scheduling and publishing
need a paid plan. Check your plan at
[app.tryunsora.com](https://app.tryunsora.com).

**An account is missing.** Claude only sees accounts connected in the Unsora web
app. Connect the account there, then ask Claude again.

**A post is rejected with an `issues` list.** The media or caption breaks a
platform rule, such as video length, image count, or caption length. Ask Claude
to adjust the post, or fix it in the composer.

**A post is partly published.** Ask Claude to retry it. Retrying only re-attempts
the accounts that failed and never posts twice to the same account.

**Clipping can't read my video.** The video must be at a public URL. To use a
file from your computer, upload it in the Unsora web app first, then ask Claude
to clip it from your library.

## Data and privacy

The connector reads and writes only your Unsora account data, through the
Unsora API, using the account you sign in with. It doesn't read your Claude
conversation history or files. See the
[Unsora privacy policy](https://tryunsora.com/privacy) for how data is
collected, used, and retained.
