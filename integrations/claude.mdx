title: "Unsora for Claude"
description: "Connect Unsora to Claude to turn long videos, podcasts, and streams into short clips, then post or schedule them to your social accounts."
---

The Unsora connector lets Claude turn long videos into short clips and post
them for you. Give Claude a podcast, stream, webinar, or interview, and Unsora
finds the strongest moments and cuts them into Shorts, Reels, and TikToks,
reframed to the aspect ratio you choose and with captions if you want them. You
can also ask for specific moments in plain language, such as "every time we
talk about pricing".

When your clips are ready, Claude can post them for you. You can publish now,
schedule for later, or send one clip to several platforms at once. Claude can
also show you how your posts are performing.

The connector works in Claude on the web, Claude Desktop, Claude mobile, and
Claude Code.

<Note>
  This connector has Unsora's clipping and scheduling tools only. For video,
  image, music, voiceover, and avatar generation too, connect the full
  [Unsora MCP server](/integrations/mcp) at `https://mcp.tryunsora.com/mcp`.
</Note>

## What you need

- An Unsora account. Sign up at [tryunsora.com](https://tryunsora.com).
- Unsora credits for clipping.
- To post clips, you need a paid Unsora plan and at least one social account
  connected in the [Unsora web app](https://app.tryunsora.com). Unsora supports
  YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, Pinterest, Threads, Bluesky,
  X, and Google Business Profile.

<Warning>
  Only clip videos you own or have permission to use.
</Warning>

## Connect Unsora to Claude

1. In Claude, open **Settings** > **Connectors** and select **Browse connectors**.
2. Search for **Unsora** and select **Connect**.
3. Sign in to your Unsora account and approve access.

Unsora's tools are now available in your conversations. To turn the connector
on or off for a conversation, open the **+** menu and select **Connectors**.

<Note>
  On Team and Enterprise plans, an organization Owner may need to enable the
  connector before members can use it.
</Note>

### Add it by URL

If Unsora isn't listed in the directory, add it as a custom connector:

1. Open **Settings** > **Connectors** and select **Add custom connector**.
2. Enter `https://claude-mcp.tryunsora.com/mcp` as the URL.
3. Select **Add**, then **Connect**, and sign in to Unsora.

### Claude Code

Run:

```bash
claude mcp add --transport http unsora https://claude-mcp.tryunsora.com/mcp
```

Then run `/mcp` in Claude Code, select **unsora**, and choose
**Authenticate**.

## Example prompts

- "Clip this podcast into five 30-second vertical clips with captions: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VIDEO_ID"
- "Find every moment where we talk about pricing in this webinar and make 9:16 clips: https://example.com/webinar.mp4"
- "Cut this stream into highlights for YouTube Shorts."
- "Schedule the second clip to TikTok and Instagram tomorrow at 9am with the caption 'Pricing, explained in 30 seconds'."
- "How did my posts do over the last 30 days?"

In Claude on the web and in Claude Desktop, Unsora shows interactive panels in
the conversation. You can watch clips play, finish a post in the composer, and
browse your posts, library, and analytics.

## Add a video

Claude can clip a video from any of these sources:

- **A YouTube link or a direct video file URL**, such as a link to an `.mp4`
  file. Page links from TikTok, Instagram, or Vimeo don't work. Download those
  videos and upload the file instead.
- **A file on your computer.** Ask Claude to open your Unsora library, then
  select **Upload** in the library panel. The file goes straight to Unsora's
  storage through a one-time upload link, and Claude never reads the file
  itself.
- **A file in your Unsora library.** Ask Claude to use a clip or upload you
  already have.

In Claude Code, Claude can upload a local file for you with a one-time upload
link.

## Choose clip settings

Clipping spends credits, so Claude confirms these settings before it starts:

| Setting | Options |
| ------- | ------- |
| Aspect ratio | 9:16 for Shorts, Reels, and TikTok; 1:1 or 4:5 for feed posts; 16:9 for YouTube; or the original ratio. Unsora reframes the video to keep the speaker in the center. |
| Captions | Off, or on with one of 35 caption styles (below). Choosing a style turns captions on. The default style is `classic-yellow`. |
| Number of clips | 1 to 20 |
| Clip length | Under 30 seconds, 30–60 seconds, 60–90 seconds, 90 seconds to 3 minutes, 3 to 5 minutes, or automatic (up to 90 seconds) |
| Moments to find | Optional. Describe the moments you want instead of choosing a length. When set, the clip length is ignored. |
| Translation | Optional. Set `targetLang` to translate captions, and `sourceLang` if the spoken language needs a hint. |

Caption styles: `classic-yellow`, `white-card`, `black-box`, `bold-white`,
`white-underline`, `playful-green`, `sketch-blue`, `bubble-blue`,
`bubble-green`, `glow-yellow`, `duo-green`, `duo-purple`, `elegant-purple`,
`neon-cyan`, `glow-pink`, `bold-yellow`, `soft-orange`, `glow-green`,
`glow-orange`, `retro-outline`, `mint-bubble`, `comic-duo`, `static-outline`,
`static-comic`, `static-minimal`, `static-green`, `static-yellow`,
`static-orange`, `gaming-magenta`, `gaming-green`, `gaming-yellow`,
`gaming-white`, `gaming-purple-box`, `gaming-cyan`, and `gaming-orange-box`.

Long videos take several minutes to process. The clips panel updates by
itself, and you can ask Claude to check the job at any time.

## Post and schedule clips

Claude creates one post for each clip. For each post, you choose the caption,
the accounts, and when to publish:

- **Publish now.** Unsora posts the clip to your accounts right away.
- **Schedule.** Unsora publishes the clip at the time you set.
- **Save as a draft.** You can finish the post later.

A post can go to up to 10 accounts. Times can include a timezone, and you can
give one account its own caption or title. To post several images as a
carousel, ask Claude for a slideshow post.

Where Claude can show panels, it opens the post composer with your caption,
clip, accounts, and time already filled in. You can set options for each
platform there, or ask Claude to set them:

- **YouTube:** title, visibility, tags, category, and made for kids.
- **TikTok:** privacy, comments, duets, stitches, branded content, the
  AI-generated label, auto-added music, and the cover.
- **Instagram:** the Reel cover image.
- **Pinterest:** board, pin title (up to 100 characters), and link.
- **Google Business Profile:** a button (Learn more, Book, Order, Shop, Sign
  up, or Call) and its link.

Once a post is scheduled, you can change its caption, time, timezone, and
accounts, or turn it back into a draft. To change the media, delete the post
and create a new one.

If a post fails on some accounts, ask Claude to retry it. Unsora only retries
the accounts that failed and never posts twice to an account that succeeded.

## Tools

The connector provides these tools. Claude asks for your approval before it
runs a tool that changes data, unless you've allowed that tool to run without
asking.

| Tool | What it does | Type |
| ---- | ------------ | ---- |
| `create_clipping` | Cuts a long video into short clips. Spends credits. | Write |
| `wait_for_clipping` | Checks a clipping job until it finishes, every 30 seconds by default | Read-only |
| `list_clips` | Lists your past clipping jobs and their clips | Read-only |
| `get_credits` | Shows your credit balance | Read-only |
| `get_subscription` | Shows your plan and whether posting is enabled | Read-only |
| `get_accounts` | Lists your connected social accounts | Read-only |
| `compose_post` | Opens the post composer, which you finish yourself | Write |
| `create_post` | Saves a draft, schedules a post, or publishes it now | Write |
| `publish_post` | Publishes a draft or scheduled post now | Write |
| `update_post` | Edits or reschedules a draft or scheduled post | Write, overwrites |
| `retry_post` | Retries only the accounts that failed on a post | Write |
| `delete_post` | Deletes a post from Unsora | Destructive |
| `get_post` / `list_posts` | Reads your posts and their publishing status. `list_posts` filters by status and pages through results. | Read-only |
| `get_post_analytics` | Shows views, likes, comments, and shares over 7 to 90 days (30 by default). It can refresh the numbers from the platforms first. | Read-only |
| `list_uploads` | Lists your uploaded files and opens the **Upload** panel | Read-only |
| `upload_file` | Imports a file from a public URL (up to 200 MB) into your library | Write |
| `create_upload_url` / `complete_upload` | Uploads a local file through a one-time link | Write |
| `delete_library_item` | Permanently deletes a clipping job or uploaded file | Destructive |

The interactive panels also use three read-only helper tools that Claude
doesn't call directly. `clipping_status` updates clipping progress,
`pinterest_boards` lists your Pinterest boards, and `tiktok_creator_info`
loads your TikTok posting options.

### Tool inputs

| Tool | Inputs |
| ---- | ------ |
| `create_clipping` | `videoUrl`, `ratio` (`9:16`, `1:1`, `4:5`, `16:9`, `original`), `enableCaption`, `captionStyle`, `limit` (1–20), `targetDuration` (`auto`, `lt30`, `30-60`, `60-90`, `90-3min`, `gt3min`), `query`, `sourceLang`, `targetLang` |
| `wait_for_clipping` | `clippingId`, `maxAttempts` (1–240), `intervalSeconds` (2–60, default 30) |
| `create_post` | `caption`, `accountIds` (1–10), `mediaType` (`video`, `slideshow`, `none`), `mediaUrl` / `mediaUrls`, `coverUrl`, `scheduled_at`, `timezone`, `publishNow`, `external_id`, `title`, `accountOverrides`, and the `instagram`, `tiktok`, `youtube`, `pinterest`, and `google_business` settings above |
| `update_post` | `postId`, `caption`, `scheduled_at` (`null` turns the post back into a draft), `timezone`, `accountIds` |
| `list_posts` | `status` (`DRAFT`, `SCHEDULED`, `PUBLISHING`, `PUBLISHED`, `PARTIALLY_PUBLISHED`, `FAILED`), `page`, `limit` (up to 100) |
| `get_post_analytics` | `days` (7–90, default 30), `refresh` |
| `list_uploads` / `list_clips` | `page`, `limit` (up to 100) |
| `upload_file` | `url`, `fileName`, `contentType` |
| `complete_upload` | `blobName`, `fileName` |

Every tool declares MCP annotations (`readOnlyHint`, `destructiveHint`, and
`openWorldHint`), so Claude can tell read-only tools apart from tools that
change your data or publish outside Unsora.

<Note>
  Deleting a post in Unsora doesn't remove content that's already published on
  a platform. Deleting a clip or upload from your library can't be undone.
</Note>

## Security and permissions

This section is for IT admins who review the connector.

- **Remote server only.** The connector is a hosted MCP server at
  `https://claude-mcp.tryunsora.com/mcp`, using the Streamable HTTP transport.
  It doesn't install or run any code on your computer.
- **OAuth sign-in.** Each user signs in with their own Unsora account through
  OAuth 2.1, and Claude registers itself as an OAuth client automatically. The
  connector requests the `openid`, `profile`, `email`, `public_metadata`,
  `private_metadata`, and `offline_access` scopes. These identify the user's
  Unsora account and keep the connection signed in.
- **Scoped to one account.** The connector can only read and change data in the
  Unsora account the user signed in with. It uses the same Unsora API and
  permissions as the [Unsora web app](https://app.tryunsora.com).
- **No posting without a request.** Unsora publishes only to the social
  accounts the user chooses, at the time the user asks for. Users connect and
  manage their social accounts in the Unsora web app, not in Claude.
- **API keys.** As an alternative to OAuth, you can send an Unsora API key in
  the `Authorization: Bearer`, `x-api-key`, or `apiKey` header. Treat API keys like passwords. See
  [Get started](/get-started) to create one.
- **Revoking access.** To disconnect, open **Settings** > **Connectors** in
  Claude, select **Unsora**, and choose **Disconnect**. To revoke an API key,
  delete it in the Unsora dashboard.

### Data the connector sends to Unsora

When you use the connector, Claude sends Unsora the details needed for each
request:

- video links and files you upload
- clip settings
- captions
- the accounts you choose
- schedule times

Unsora doesn't receive your conversation history or any files you share with
Claude in the conversation. See the
[privacy policy](https://tryunsora.com/privacy) to learn how Unsora collects,
uses, and keeps data, and read the [terms of service](https://tryunsora.com/terms).

## Troubleshooting

**Sign-in doesn't finish, or Claude says the connector isn't authorized.**
Open **Settings** > **Connectors**, select **Unsora**, and choose
**Disconnect**. Then connect again and finish the Unsora sign-in in the window
that opens.

**Claude says the video link isn't supported.** Clipping only accepts YouTube
links and direct video file URLs. For TikTok, Instagram, or Vimeo videos,
download the file and upload it with the library's **Upload** button.

**Clips are still processing.** Long videos take several minutes. The clips
panel updates by itself. You can also ask Claude to check the job again.

**Clipping fails because you're out of credits.** Ask Claude to check your
credits, or check them at [app.tryunsora.com](https://app.tryunsora.com).

**A post fails with `PLAN_REQUIRED`.** Scheduling and publishing need a paid
plan, so Unsora returns a 403 error. Check your plan at
[app.tryunsora.com](https://app.tryunsora.com).

**An account is missing.** Claude only sees the accounts you connected in the
Unsora web app. Connect the account there, then ask Claude again.

**A post is rejected with a list of issues.** The media or caption breaks a
platform rule, such as the video length, file size, image count, or caption
length. The error names the rule for each platform. Ask Claude to adjust the
post, or fix it in the composer.

**A post is only partly published.** Ask Claude to retry it. Unsora only
retries the accounts that failed.
